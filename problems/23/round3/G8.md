## 1. PROVED — And(k) is the circular complete graph K_{(3k-1)/k}

`G8_struct.py`. The multiplier v ↦ kv mod (3k-1) is an isomorphism
And(k) → K_{(3k-1)/k} (vertices Z_p, i~j iff k ≤ |i-j|_p ≤ p-k), p = 3k-1.
Proof: k·(3j+1) = 3jk + k ≡ k + j (mod 3k-1) because 3k ≡ 1, so the connection set
{1,4,...,3k-2} is carried bijectively onto {k, k+1, ..., 2k-1}. Verified exactly k=2..7.

**Consequence 1 (monotonicity).** K_{p/q} → K_{p'/q'} iff p/q ≤ p'/q', and
(3k-1)/k = 3 - 1/k is strictly increasing, so
    And(2) → And(3) → And(4) → …
Since H → K implies max psi(H) ≤ max psi(K), the sequence
    **A(k) := max_x psi(And(k),x)   is NONDECREASING in k, with A(2) = 1/25.**
Hence a counterexample at one k propagates to all larger k, and the family question
is a single limit question, not 
infinitely many separate ones.

**Consequence 2 (no homomorphism route for k ≥ 3).** χ_f(And(k)) = n/α = (3k-1)/k > 5/2
for k ≥ 3, so And(k) ↛ C5 and the "compose with a hom to C5" certificate is
unavailable for every k ≥ 3. (This is why And(3) is the first hard case.)

## 2. PROVED — the whole family is one continuum object

Let Γ be the graph on the circle R/Z with x ~ y iff circular distance d(x,y) > 1/3.
Γ is triangle-free (three arcs each > 1/3 cannot sum to 1). For p = 3k-1 equally
spaced points, d(i,j) > 1/3 ⟺ |i-j| > p/3 = k - 1/3 ⟺ |i-j| ≥ k, so

    **And(k) = Γ restricted to 3k-1 equally spaced points, exactly.**

So sup_k A(k) is the finite-configuration version of max over probability measures μ
on the circle of psi(Γ,μ), and a proof there covers every And(k) at once (and every
"circular threshold" triangle-free graph). `G8_circle.py` runs the discretised version.

## 3. PROVED — support/face reduction, and the And(3) boundary

For every graph H and every x, `psi(H,x) = psi(H[supp x], x|supp)`: a cut of H
restricts to a cut of H[supp x] and every cut of H[supp x] extends to H, and vertices
of weight 0 contribute nothing. Hence if H[W] → C5 then psi(H,x) ≤ 1/25 for all x
supported in W (accepted fact 2).

`G8_struct.py` (exhaustive over all induced subgraphs, exact backtracking hom search):
**every proper induced subgraph of And(3) admits a homomorphism to C5**
(0 obstructions on 5, 6, 7 vertices; the only obstruction is And(3) itself). Therefore

    **THEOREM. psi(And(3), x) ≤ 1/25 for every x on the simplex that is not of full
    support. The whole of the And(3) case reduces to full-support x.**

`G8_obstr2.py`, exhaustive over all 2^n induced subgraphs of And(k), k = 3,4,5:
**And(k)[W] → C5 ⟺ W contains no induced copy of the Wagner graph C8(1,4)**
(0 mismatches out of 256 / 2048 / 16384). All 11 (resp. 63) minimal obstructions in
And(4) (resp. And(5)) are isomorphic to And(3). So And(3) is *the* obstruction in the
whole family.

## 6. NEW CERTIFICATE CLASS, and exactly where it dies

### 6.1 The scheme (`G8_certlp.py`)

An **atom** is (S, A, B): S a cut, A ∩ B = ∅, every monochromatic edge of S having one
end in A and one in B. Then q_S(x) ≤ x(A)·x(B). With weights w_j ≥ 0, Σ w_j = 1,

    psi(x) ≤ min_j q_{S_j}(x) ≤ min_j x(A_j)x(B_j)
           ≤ Π_j (x(A_j)x(B_j))^{w_j}                 (min ≤ weighted geometric mean)
           = (Π_j x(A_j)^{w_j})(Π_j x(B_j)^{w_j})
           ≤ (Σ_j w_j x(A_j))(Σ_j w_j x(B_j))          (weighted AM–GM, twice).

So the LINEAR system  Σ_j w_j [v ∈ A_j] = 1/5 = Σ_j w_j [v ∈ B_j] (all v), Σ w_j = 1
proves max psi ≤ 1/25. The averaging here is **geometric**, so the recorded dead fact
"any fixed distribution over cuts gives ≥ 1/20 on C5" (which is about *arithmetic*
averaging) does not apply. Indeed on C5 the LP is feasible and returns the sharp
certificate: the 5 single-edge cuts, A = {i}, B = {i+1}, w = 1/5 — value exactly 1/25.

### 6.2 BLOCKED for every k ≥ 3, with an exact witness (`G8_monostruct.py`, `G8_block.py`)

**Lemma (matching bound).** If L, L' are nonnegative linear forms with L(x)L'(x) ≥ q_S(x)
on the nonnegative orthant, and ν = ν(mono(S)) is the maximum matching of the
monochromatic graph, then (Σ_v l_v)(Σ_v l'_v) ≥ ν².
*Proof.* For a monochromatic edge ab put x = e_a + e_b: q_S(x) ≥ 1, so
(l_a+l_b)(l'_a+l'_b) ≥ 1. For a matching a_1b_1,…,a_νb_ν the 2ν vertices are distinct,
so by Cauchy–Schwarz
(Σl)(Σl') ≥ (Σ_i(l_{a_i}+l_{b_i}))(Σ_i(l'_{a_i}+l'_{b_i})) ≥ (Σ_i √((l_{a_i}+l_{b_i})(l'_{a_i}+l'_{b_i})))² ≥ ν². ∎

Evaluating the certificate chain at the uniform point x = (1/n,…,1/n) gives
n^{-2}Π_j(λ_jλ'_j)^{w_j} ≤ 1/25, so **some atom must have λλ' ≤ n²/25**.

Exhaustive over all cuts (k = 2,3,4,5): min over cuts of ν(mono(S)) = k-1, and no cut
of And(k), k ≥ 3, has a star as its monochromatic set. Hence every atom has
λλ' ≥ (k-1)², while n²/25 = (3k-1)²/25, and

    25(k-1)² > (3k-1)²  ⟺  5(k-1) > 3k-1  ⟺  k > 2.

| k | min ν(mono) | required λλ' ≤ n²/25 | forced λλ' ≥ ν² | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 | POSSIBLE — and attained (sharp C5 certificate) |
| 3 | 2 | 64/25 = 2.56 | 4 | **BLOCKED** |
| 4 | 3 | 121/25 = 4.84 | 9 | **BLOCKED** |
| 5 | 4 | 196/25 = 7.84 | 16 | **BLOCKED** |

C5 sits exactly on the boundary (1 = 1); every Andrásfai graph beyond it is strictly
outside. The LP of §6.1 is confirmed infeasible for And(3) over all 3496 atoms.

### 6.3 BLOCKED for k ≥ 4 — no fixed family works at all (`G8_block.py`, `G8_block2.py`)

Any certificate of the shape psi(x) ≤ min_j q_{S_j}(x) ≤ Π_j q_{S_j}(x)^{w_j} ≤ 1/25
(Σw_j = 1) forces q_{S_j}(x*) = 1/25 for every j in supp(w) and every maximiser x*,
because otherwise the geometric mean at x* exceeds min = 1/25. So supp(w) lies in the
intersection of the active cut sets over all maximisers. Every induced C5 of And(k),
uniformly weighted, is a maximiser (accepted fact 3). Exhaustive computation:

* And(4): 33 induced C5s, **intersection of active cut sets = ∅**.
* And(5): 98 induced C5s, **intersection = ∅**.

**Verbatim blocking lemma:** *there is no cut S of And(4) with q_S(x*) = 1/25
simultaneously for all 33 induced-C5 uniform points x*.* Hence NO fixed distribution
over cuts — arithmetic or geometric — can certify max psi(And(k)) ≤ 1/25 for k ≥ 4.
Any proof for k ≥ 4 must select the cut family as a function of x (e.g. by the support
reduction of §3).

## 7. And(3): reduction to ONE explicit polynomial inequality

For And(3) the intersection of §6.3 is **not** empty. Over all 16 exact C5-blow-up
maximisers of psi (all surjective homs W → C5 for induced W, uniform and extreme
splittings, each verified to have psi exactly 1/25) exactly **five** cuts survive
(`G8_block2.py`). With Wagner as Z_8, cycle edges i~i+1 and rungs i~i+4:

    q1 = a0a7 + a3a4      (cycle edges e3, e7 — an antipodal pair)
    q2 = a1a2 + a5a6      (e1, e5)
    q3 = a0a1 + a4a5      (e0, e4)
    q4 = a2a3 + a6a7      (e2, e6)
    q5 = a0a4 + a1a5 + a2a6 + a3a7    (all four rungs; the alternating cut of C8)

`G8_w5quick.py`, `G8_wagner5.cpp`, `G8_reduce.py`:

* max over the simplex of min(q1,…,q5) = 0.039999996 (numeric, 400 restarts);
* max over the simplex of q1q2q3q4q5 = 1.024000e-07 = **25^{-5} to 9 digits**;
* EXACT integer check, all a ≥ 0 with Σa = q, every q ≤ 32:
  `25·min_j q_j(a) ≤ q²`, equality exactly at 5 | q. Zero violations.

**Reduced coordinates (exact identity, verified on 400 random rational points).**
Put m_i = a_i + a_{i+4}, a_i = m_i(1+c_i)/2, a_{i+4} = m_i(1-c_i)/2 (i = 0,1,2,3),
so m ≥ 0, c ∈ [-1,1]^4, Σm_i = Σa_v. Then identically

    q3 = m0m1(1+c0c1)/2,  q2 = m1m2(1+c1c2)/2,  q4 = m2m3(1+c2c3)/2,
    q1 = m3m0(1 − c3c0)/2,          <-- the Möbius twist: a MINUS sign
    q5 = Σ_i m_i²(1−c_i²)/4,
    q1q2q3q4q5 = (m0m1m2m3)² · P(c) · S(m,c) / 64,
    P(c) = (1+c0c1)(1+c1c2)(1+c2c3)(1−c3c0),   S = Σ_i m_i²(1−c_i²).

The odd number of minus signs around the 4-cycle is precisely the obstruction to
C5-colourability: the four factors of P cannot be simultaneously maximised, since that
would need sign(c0c1)·sign(c1c2)·sign(c2c3)·sign(c3c0) = −1 while the left side is a
product of squares = +1. (This is the algebraic form of "Wagner is the obstruction".)

**TERMINAL LEMMA (NOT PROVED).**

    (m0m1m2m3)² · (1+c0c1)(1+c1c2)(1+c2c3)(1−c3c0) · Σ_i m_i²(1−c_i²)  ≤  64 / 5^10
    for all m ≥ 0 with Σ m_i = 1 and all c ∈ [-1,1]^4.

Equivalently q1q2q3q4q5 ≤ (Σa)^{10}/5^{10} on the nonnegative orthant. It is TIGHT on a
positive-dimensional variety: e.g. (m,c) = ((2/5,1/5,1/5,1/5),(0,1,1,1)) and
((0.340,0.2,0.2,0.260),(0.176,1,1,0.539)); 6000 Nelder–Mead restarts on the reduced
form give max/target = 1.000000000. Granting it,

    psi(And(3),x) ≤ min_j q_j(x) ≤ (q1q2q3q4q5)^{1/5} ≤ 1/25,

which with accepted fact 3 gives max_x psi(And(3),x) = 1/25 exactly.

Two AM–GM attacks on the terminal lemma that FAIL, recorded so they are not retried:
* term-by-term AM–GM on the 64 monomials of the expanded product: the single monomial
  a0²a1²a2²a3²a4² already contributes Πα_v^{α_v} = 4^5 = 1024, which is the entire
  budget 2^{10}, and 63 positive terms remain;
* the antipodal-folding bound q_j ≤ u_ju_{j+1} (u_i = a_i + a_{i+4}) together with
  q5 ≤ Σu_i²/4 gives 1/1048576 = 9.5e-7 against the needed 1.024e-7 — lossy by 9.3x;
* the split max_m[(Πm)²Σw_im_i²] ≤ (Σw_i)·16/5^{10} is false as a certificate: it needs
  P(c)·Σ_i(1−c_i²) ≤ 4, violated at c_i = √(1/5) for all i (value 4.424).

## 8. Recorded consistency with the DEAD list

The rotation-averaging over half-circle arcs of Γ gives, exactly,
E_θ[mono(A_θ)] = Σ_{far pairs}(1−2d(u,v)) x_u x_v, i.e. the kernel
K(t) = |1−2t|·1[1/3 < t < 2/3] on the circle. On the uniform C5 measure this is exactly
1/25, but its maximum over the simplex is 1/20 (two atoms at distance 2/5, mass 1/2
each). That reproduces the recorded dead fact for arithmetic averaging from a new
direction and confirms that only min/geometric-mean arguments can reach 1/25.

# G10 — counterexample hunt in the psi landscape (Erdős #23)

Target: a triangle-free `H` and `x` in the simplex with
`psi(H,x) = min_{cuts S} sum_{uv monochromatic} x_u x_v > 1/25`.
Equivalently integer `a >= 0`, `q = sum a`, with `bip(H[a]) > q^2/25`.

Everything below is exact (64-bit integers / Fractions). Floats only steered searches.

---

## 1. PROVED (with proofs)

### G10-0 (edge monotonicity; WLOG maximal)
Adding an edge `uv` to `H` adds the nonnegative term `x_u x_v` to `Q_c` for every cut `c`
in which `u,v` are on the same side and `0` otherwise; so every `Q_c` weakly increases and
`psi(H,x) <= psi(H',x)` for `H ⊆ H'` on the same vertex set. Hence a maximal
triangle-free completion never lowers `max_x psi`, and the search over **maximal**
triangle-free patterns on `h` vertices covers **all** triangle-free patterns on `h` vertices.

### G10-1 (homomorphism cap)
**If `H -> C5` then `max_x psi(H,x) <= 1/25`.**
Proof. Let `φ : H -> C5`. For a cut `S` of `C5` put `T = φ^{-1}(S)`. Every `T`-monochromatic
edge `uv` of `H` maps to an `S`-monochromatic edge of `C5` (`φ(u) ≠ φ(v)`, no loops), and
distinct `H`-edges give distinct terms of the expansion of
`sum_{ij S-mono} y_i y_j`, `y_i = sum_{φ(v)=i} x_v`. All terms are `>= 0`, so
`Q_T(x) <= Q_S(y)`. Minimising, `psi(H,x) <= psi(C5,y) <= 1/25`. ∎

Consequence used throughout: **only patterns with NO homomorphism to C5 can matter.**
Counts of maximal triangle-free graphs and of the non-`C5`-colourable ones:

|---|---|---|

| 9 | 16 | 2 |

Since `h <= 7` gives **zero** non-`C5`-colourable maximal triangle-free graphs:
**`max_x psi(H,x) <= 1/25` is PROVED unconditionally for every triangle-free pattern `H`
on at most 7 vertices**, i.e. `bip(H[a]) <= (sum a)^2/25` for all such `H` and all integer `a`.
(Every triangle-free graph is a spanning subgraph of a maximal triangle-free one on the same
vertex set, and a subgraph of a `C5`-colourable graph is `C5`-colourable.)
Confirmed independently by enumerating **all** triangle-free graphs, connected or not
(`geng -t`), with a second, separately written hom-to-`C5` solver:
`n = 2..7 -> 2, 3, 7, 14, 38, 107 graphs, 0 non-C5-colourable`; the first exceptions appear at
`n = 8` (4 of 410, the maximal one among them being the Wagner graph `V8 = C8(1,4)`).

### G10-2 (every non-bipartite maximal triangle-free graph has odd girth exactly 5)
Maximal triangle-free ⟹ every non-adjacent pair has a common neighbour ⟹ diameter `<= 2`.
If the shortest odd cycle had length `2k+1 >= 7`, two of its vertices at cycle-distance `3`
would have a common neighbour `z`, producing a closed walk of length `5`, hence an odd cycle
of length `3` or `5` — contradiction. ∎
So the 1/25 plateau (accepted fact 3) is present in **every** non-bipartite maximal
triangle-free host: there is no "odd-girth-7 corner" of the conjecture to hide in.

### G10-3 (no first-order ascent from any C5 point, in any host)
Let `H` be triangle-free with an induced `C5 = v_1..v_5`, `x*` the concentration point
(`1/5` on the C5). Admissible direction: `sum d = 0`, `d_w >= 0` off the C5. Active cuts are
exactly those with **one** monochromatic C5-edge. Writing `A_w = N(w) ∩ C5` (independent in
C5, so `|A_w| <= 2`), `P_j = {v_j, v_{j+2}}`, `D_j = sum_{w : A_w = P_j} d_w`, the minimum of
`5·(directional derivative)` over the free sides of the outside vertices is

```
F_i(d) = d_i + d_{i+1} + D_i + D_{i-1}          (i = 1..5, indices mod 5)
```

and

```
F_1 + F_2 + F_3 + F_4 + F_5  =  -2 * sum_{w : |A_w| <= 1} d_w   <=  0 .
```

(Each of the five diagonal pairs is split by exactly two of the five active cut classes;
`sum_i (d_i+d_{i+1}) = 2 sum_i d_i = -2 sum_w d_w`.)
Hence the `F_i` cannot all be positive: **`x*` is first-order stationary in every triangle-free
host.** This is why every hill-climb in this project has reported exactly `1/25`.

### G10-4 (the flat face is C5-colourable — so the whole neighbourhood is capped)
If `F_1 = ... = F_5 = 0` then `d_w = 0` whenever `|A_w| <= 1`, so every `w` with `d_w > 0`
has exactly two C5-neighbours, `A_w = P_j` for some `j`. Two such vertices `w, w'` can be
adjacent only if `A_w ∩ A_{w'} = ∅` (else triangle), and `P_j ∩ P_k = ∅` iff `k = j ± 1`.
Therefore `w ↦ j+1` together with `v_i ↦ i` is a homomorphism
`H[C5 ∪ {w : d_w > 0}] -> C5`. By G10-1, `psi(H,y) <= 1/25` for **every** `y` supported there.
∎

**Combined content of G10-3 + G10-4:** "spreading weight off a single induced C5" cannot
beat `1/25` — every admissible direction either strictly decreases `psi` at first order or
moves inside a `C5`-colourable face on which `psi <= 1/25` identically. **A counterexample
must be a separate local maximum, not reachable by any straight ray from a C5 point.**
Exact verification: `G10_c5point.py` — closed form vs brute force over all active cuts on
250 random triangle-free hosts (0 mismatches), G10-3 identity (0 failures), 2481 explicitly
constructed flat directions all C5-colourable and all with `psi <= 1/25` along the whole ray
(0 violations).

### G10-5 (averaging certificates are blocked *locally*, not just globally)
Let `λ` be ANY probability distribution over the cuts of `H` and `f(x) = sum_c λ_c Q_c(x)`
(an upper bound for `psi`). Then for every neighbourhood `U` of a C5-concentration point,
`sup_U f > 1/25`.
Proof. `f(x*) = (1/25) sum_c λ_c k_c`, `k_c = #`mono C5-edges `>= 1`, so `f(x*) = 1/25` forces
`λ` supported on the active cuts. Inside the C5 face the derivative is
`(1/5) sum_i μ_i (d_i + d_{i+1})` with `μ_i` the mass on mono-edge class `i`; it vanishes for
all `d` with `sum d = 0` iff `μ_{i-1} + μ_i` is constant, i.e. (odd cycle) `μ_i = 1/5`. The
second-order term is then `(1/5) sum_i d_i d_{i+1}`, and the explicit integer direction
`d = (309, -809, -809, 309, 1000)` has `sum d = 0`, `sum_i d_i d_{i+1} = 772519 > 0`. ∎
Verified exactly in `G10_avgblock.py`: `f(x*) = 1/25`, second-order coefficient `772519/5`,
and e.g. at `t = 1/100000`, `f = 2000772519/50000000000 > 1/25` while
`psi = 368294481/10000000000 <= 1/25`.
**This strictly generalises the recorded dead end "fixed averaging certificates give >= 1/20
on C5" (that is the one-global-`λ` case) to every region-decomposition / branch-and-bound
scheme whose per-region certificate is a convex combination of cut values — including the
one-cut-per-region bound. No such scheme can close any region containing a C5 point.**

## 2. REFUTED (my own sub-idea, killed by an explicit witness)

**Idea:** by fact 7 (Häggkvist) a counterexample `G` has `δ(G) <= floor(3N/8)`; the C5 point
has weighted degree `2/5 = 0.4 > 3/8`, so restrict the psi search to
`min_{i in supp} d_x(i) <= 3/8`, which removes the plateau and yields a genuine margin.
This is implemented (`G10_hunt2.cpp`) and at `q = 240` gives, exactly,

| pattern | constrained max (exact) | decimal | vs 1/25 |
|---|---|---|---|
| C5 | `5/128` | 0.0390625 | below |
| Petersen | `47/1200` | 0.0391667 | below |
| Grötzsch | `767/19200` | 0.0399479 | below |
| Chvátal | `767/19200` | 0.0399479 | below |

**REFUTED as a valid restriction.** Exact witness: in the Grötzsch graph take
`a = (k,k,k,k,k)` on the C5 `v_0..v_4`, `a = 1` on the apex, `0` on the five Mycielski copies,
`q = 5k+1`. The apex is in the support and its weighted degree is `0 <= floor(3q/8)`, so the
constraint holds, while `bip = k^2 = (q-1)^2/25 -> q^2/25`. The constrained supremum is
therefore still exactly `1/25`; the margins above are pure finite-`q` grid artefacts.
More generally any counterexample can be padded with a zero-weight class of a larger maximal
triangle-free host, so **Häggkvist's degree bound puts no restriction at all on the psi
landscape.** Do not re-propose it.

## 4. WHERE A COUNTEREXAMPLE CAN STILL HIDE (sharpened)

G10-3 + G10-4 remove the entire ray-neighbourhood of every C5 point in every host, and
G10-1 removes every `C5`-colourable pattern. What is left is exactly:

> a triangle-free `H` with **no homomorphism to C5**, and a **local maximum** `x` of
> `psi(H,·)` that is not joined to any C5-concentration point by an admissible straight
> segment, with `psi(H,x) > 1/25`.

Everything tested (2693 patterns, `h <= 18`, two independent optimisers, exhaustive integer
search where feasible) has exactly one local-maximum value class, `1/25`, and its maximisers
are always C5-blow-up configurations. In particular the "spread the weight" mechanism named
in the assignment is provably (G10-3/G10-4), not merely empirically, incapable of beating
`1/25`.

## 6. BLOCKED (verbatim blocking lemmas)

* Any region-decomposition / branch-and-bound certificate whose per-region bound is
  `psi(x) <= sum_c λ_c Q_c(x)` for a region-constant distribution `λ` over cuts.
  Blocking lemma (G10-5, proved above): *"For every probability distribution `λ` over the cuts
  of `H` and every neighbourhood `U` of a C5-concentration point `x*`,
  `sup_{x in U} sum_c λ_c Q_c(x) > 1/25`."*
* The degree-constrained (Häggkvist) restriction of the psi search.
  Blocking witness: *"Grötzsch, `a = (k,k,k,k,k)` on the C5, `a = 1` on the apex, `0` on the
  five copies: the apex lies in the support with weighted degree `0 <= floor(3q/8)`, and
  `bip = k^2 = (q-1)^2/25`."*

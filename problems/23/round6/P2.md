# P2 — BREAK the criterion of item 7

**VERDICT: the criterion of item 7 is FALSE, and it is false by a factor of 25/18.**
`sup_mu CRIT(mu) = 1/18` exactly (proved below, with an explicit attaining family).
This is a **CRITERION FALSIFIER**, not a counterexample: every witness below has
`ARCBOUND <= 1/32` and `psi <= 1/32` (four of them have `ARCBOUND = psi = 0`), so neither the
arc-cut conjecture nor Erdős 23 is touched. What dies is the reduction of item 7 — and with it the

Everything below is verified twice in exact rational arithmetic by two independently written
implementations (`P2_verify.py`, `P2_verify2.py` — different data structures, different formulas,

## 1. The ceiling: `CRIT < 1/18` always, and `1/18` is attained in the limit

Two one-line facts:

* every adjacent pair has `d > 1/3`, so `T > W/3` and therefore
  **`A = W − 2T < W/3`**;
* Cauchy–Schwarz gives `∫g² dmu = 4W² + Var_mu(g) >= 4W²`, so
  **`bound_0 = W − ∫g²dmu = W − 4W² − Var_mu(g) <= W − 4W²`**.

Hence for every probability measure on the circle

```
      CRIT(mu)  <  min( W/3 ,  W − 4W² )   <=   1/18 ,
```

the two branches crossing at `W = 1/6` where both equal `1/18 = 0.0555556 = 1.38889 × (1/25)`.
So the criterion of item 7 can only be false, and it can be false by at most the factor `25/18`.
It is false by exactly that factor: the bound is approached.

## 2. The extremal falsifier: any 1/3-periodic measure (assignment (c))

> **THEOREM.** Let `mu` be an atomless 1/3-periodic probability measure on `R/Z`. Then
> `g(x) = 1/3` for **every** `x`, `W = 1/6`, `Var_mu(g) = 0`, `m(b) = 1/18` for **every** `b`, and
> therefore `bound_k = 1/18` for **every** `k >= 0`. Consequently `CRIT(mu) = min(A, 1/18) = A`.

*Proof.* The far set of `x` is the open arc `(x+1/3, x+2/3)` of length exactly `1/3` = one period,
so its mass is `1/3` minus the atoms at its two endpoints, i.e. `1/3`. Then `W = ½∫g = 1/6`,
`m(b) = W − ∫_{N(b)} g dmu = 1/6 − (1/3)(1/3) = 1/18`, and every `bound_k` is an average of the
constant `m`. ∎

Take `mu_eta` = three uniform arcs `[0,eta] ∪ [1/3,1/3+eta] ∪ [2/3,2/3+eta]`, mass `1/3` each
(`0 < eta <= 1/6`). Exact symbolic computation (`P2_continuum.py`):

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| `W` | `1/6` (independent of `eta`) |
| `T` | `(1+eta)/18`,  `T/W = (1+eta)/3` |
| `A = W − 2T` | `(1 − 2 eta)/18` |
| `g` | `1/3` everywhere, `Var_mu(g) = 0` |
| `m(b)`, every `bound_k` | `1/18` |
| **`CRIT`** | **`(1 − 2 eta)/18`  >  1/25  ⟺  `eta < 7/50`** |
| true `ARCBOUND` | `<= 1/36` (arc = one whole cluster + half of the next) |

So **every** `eta` in `(0, 7/50)` is a falsifier — an open set of measures, not a knife edge — and
`CRIT → 1/18` as `eta → 0`, meeting the ceiling of §1. The truth is `1/36`: the criterion
overestimates the arc-cut value by a factor of exactly **2** on this family.

### Why item 6 does not stop this (the exact hole in the chain)

Item 6 concludes: *"a purely 3-fold measure has g constant and no adjacent pairs at all"*, and R5-K9
§7 uses that to claim a `g`-flat measure "has no room left". That inference holds **only for the
three-atom measure on a coset `{a, a+1/3, a+2/3}`**, where the pairs sit at distance *exactly* `1/3`,
which is not `> 1/3`. Spread each atom over an arc of length `eta` — still exactly 3-fold, still
`g ≡ 1/3` — and *half* of those pairs become strictly farther than `1/3`: `W = 1/6 > 0`. The
Fourier series of item 6 agrees, and is the independent fourth derivation of the value:

```
   A(eta→0) = psihat(0) + 2 Σ_{k>=1} psihat(3k) = 1/36 + 2·(1/72) = 1/18 ,
   because psihat(6j) = 0 and psihat(3(2j+1)) = 1/(9 pi² (2j+1)²), whose sum is (1/(9pi²))(pi²/8) = 1/72.
```

A second, independent hole: `bound_k` only ever sees `g` **on the support of `mu`**. Far-regularity
in the sense that matters (`g` constant on `supp mu`) is much weaker than `g` constant everywhere,
and is not controlled by any Fourier statement about `mu`.

## 3. The failure is structural: far-regularity collapses the whole hierarchy

If `g(b) = 2W` for all `b ∈ supp mu` then
`m(b) = W − Σ_{v∈N(b)} x_v g(v) = W − 2W·g(b) = W − 4W²` for every `b`, so

```
      bound_0 = bound_1 = … = bound_k = … = min_b m(b) = W − 4W²   for far-regular mu.
```

The hierarchy has **exactly one distinct value** on far-regular measures — adding levels is
worthless there — and that value exceeds `1/25` for every `W ∈ (1/20, 1/5)`, because
`4W² − W + 1/25` has roots `1/20` and `1/5`. All that is then left is `A`, and `A` can be as large
as `W/3`. This is precisely corner (c) of the assignment, and it is fatal.

**Consequently the falsifiers below defeat much more than the `g^k` hierarchy.** Each one has
`min_b m(b) > 1/25`: *every* neighbourhood cut `N(b)` is above `1/25`. Since every `bound_k` is a
weighted average of the numbers `m(b)`, this kills, with no truncation of `k` anywhere:

* the whole hierarchy `bound_0, bound_1, …` including `k → ∞`, and any other weighting;
* the R5-K9 Chebyshev term `E − Var_mu(m)/(max m − E)` (it is an upper bound for `min_b m(b)`,
  hence `>= min_b m(b) > 1/25`);
* the R5-K9 §10 harmonic mean `H = 1/E_mu[1/m]` (same reason);
* any future functional whose only inputs are the neighbourhood-cut values and `A`.

## 4. The minimal falsifier: FOUR atoms, and four is optimal

Positions `0, 3ε, 1/3 + 2ε, 2/3 + ε` with **uniform weights `1/4`**. The circle graph is
`2K_2` (two disjoint edges — *bipartite*, so `psi = 0`), the two edges having length `1/3 + 2ε`.

```
   W = 1/8,   g ≡ 1/4 = 2W  (far-regular),   every bound_k = min_b m(b) = 1/16 = 0.0625,
   A = 1/24 − ε/2 ,        CRIT = 1/24 − ε/2  >  1/25  ⟺  ε < 1/300 .
```

Exact witness, `ε = 1/600`, i.e. the support `{0, 9, 606, 1203}` of `Gamma_1800` with uniform weights:

```
   A = 49/1200 = 0.04083333 ,  every bound_k = 1/16 ,  CRIT = 49/1200 = 1.02083 × (1/25)
   TRUTH:  ARCBOUND = 0   and   psi = 0 .
```

The criterion certifies "at best `0.0408`" for an object whose true value is **zero**.

*Optimal four-atom weighting.* With `x = (q,p,q,p)` on those four positions, `bound_k` interpolates
monotonically (two distinct `g`-values, monotone-likelihood-ratio weights) from `bound_0` to
`bound_∞ = W − p² = q²`, while `A → (p²+q²)/3`; the two meet at `p = √2·q`, i.e. `q = (√2−1)/2`,
giving `CRIT → (3 − 2√2)/4 = 0.042893219`. Exact rational witness `(169,239,169,239)/816`,
`ε = 1/600000`:  `CRIT = 2142007159/49939200000 = 0.04289232`, `min_b m(b) = 28561/665856 > 1/25`,
`ARCBOUND = psi = 0`.

*Four is the exact minimum.* A three-atom circle graph is triangle-free, hence has `<= 2` edges:
* path `a–b–c`: `∫g² = x_b²(x_a+x_c) + x_b(x_a+x_c)² = W`, so `bound_0 = 0`;
* single edge `(a,b)` plus isolated `c`: `bound_0 = x_a x_b x_c <= 1/27 < 1/25`;
* no edge: `A = 0`.

## 8. Verification protocol

1. **`P2_verify.py`** — `Fraction` positions, `Fraction` circular distances, `W, T, A, g, m, bound_k`
   from the definitions; `ARCBOUND` by enumerating (start, length) arcs; `psi` by brute force over
   all `2^{N−1}` cuts.
2. **`P2_verify2.py`** — deliberately disjoint code path: integer positions over a common
   denominator with the integer adjacency test `3d > M`; `A` computed as the *direct* double sum
   `Σ_{u≠v, far} (1/2 − d) x_u x_v` rather than as `W − 2T`; `m(b)` computed by **building the cut
   `N(b)` as a vertex set and evaluating its monochromatic mass from scratch** (an independent
   re-derivation of item 4's identity, not a re-use of it); arcs enumerated by two independent cut
   points in the gaps. Every reported quantity of every witness is identical under both
   implementations (assertions in the script; all pass).
3. **`P2_continuum.py`** — sympy, symbolic in `eta`.
4. **Calibration**: on `C5` all four routes return exactly `1/25` for `A`, every `bound_k`,
   `ARCBOUND` and `psi`; on uniform `Gamma_m` the far-regular identity `bound_k = W − 4W²` is
   asserted for every `k`.
5. **Mandatory regression** (`P2_small.py` §D): my code reproduces the round-5
   `claude_witness_regression.arcbound` value on **all nine** witnesses (`1/49, 1/49, 1/49, 1/25,
   1/54, 3/100, 0, 1/49, 1/100` — nine exact matches), and none of the nine is a falsifier, with
   `C5` at exactly `1/25`. So the regression set is consistent with the record and simply does not
   contain a member of the failure family.

## 9. What this means for the programme, and what the repair has to look like

* The single unproved step of R5-K9 is **false**, and so is the "final form" conjecture
  `CRIT(mu) <= 1/25`, the §10 two-term form, and the ad-hoc variance form. They must be withdrawn.
  The arc-cut conjecture itself and Erdős 23 are **untouched** — every witness has
  `ARCBOUND <= 1/32`.
* The gap is not small: on the extremal family the criterion returns `1/18` while the truth is
  `1/36`. A factor of two cannot be recovered by another moment of the same objects.
* **Which cut the criterion is missing.** For `mu_eta`, the arc that certifies the truth takes *one
  whole cluster plus half of the next*: value `(2s² − 2s + 1)/18` at split fraction `s`, minimised
  at `s = 1/2` giving `1/36`. Its length is slightly more than `1/3` and **both of its endpoints lie
  in the interior of the clusters**. The neighbourhood cuts `N(b)` are exactly the `1/3`-arcs whose
  endpoints are pinned to `b ± 1/3` (i.e. to the atoms), and the half-arc family has length `1/2`.
  Both families are blind to the good cut *by construction*. Any repair must use arcs whose length
  **and** offset are free — the genuine two-parameter family of item 3 — and must be able to split
  a cluster.
* The natural next reduction, if one is wanted: bound `ARCBOUND` by the *minimum over the
  one-parameter family of `1/3⁺`-arcs with a free endpoint*, of which `N(b)` is only the subfamily
  anchored at atoms. On `mu_eta` that family already gives `1/36`.

# C41: all-relations mass audit for Erdos #424 F2

## Verdict

Let

\[
 L_2(t)=2t,\qquad L_3(t)=3t+1,\qquad L_5(t)=5t+3
\]

and let \(\Omega_k\) be the words with content

\[
 (\#2,\#3,\#5)=(15k,10k,6k).
\]

Put \(Q=2^{15}3^{10}5^6=30233088000000\). The exact conclusion is a
finite-relation route obstruction, not a result about the true number
\(D_k\) of affine offsets.

1. Every equal-content/equal-map fiber through word length 14 was enumerated,
   including every side of every identity rather than only a lexicographically
   oriented side. At exact length 14 there are 155,159 non-singleton fibers
   and 319,799 participating words. Removing words which contain a shorter
   participating factor leaves 4,777 inclusion-minimal relation sides through
   length 14.
2. Let \(\equiv_{14}\) be the contextual congruence generated by all those
   identities, and let \(C_k^{(14)}\) be its number of classes in \(\Omega_k\).
   An exact balanced circulation in the 16,652-state all-side avoidance DFA
   proves

   \[
   \liminf_{m\to\infty}
   \left(\frac{C_{64587m}^{(14)}}{Q^{64587m}}\right)^{1/(64587m)}
   \ge 1.2302522622241512.                         \tag{1}
   \]

   Hence the joint quotient by **all identities of length at most 14** is
   still exponentially larger than \(Q^k\) on an infinite subsequence. It
   cannot be \(O(Q^k/\sqrt{k})\).
3. Equation (1) proves **no quantitative lower bound for the true affine
   offset count \(D_k\)**. There is a natural surjection from the finite
   quotient to true affine maps, so the valid comparison is

   \[
                         D_k\le C_k^{(14)},           \tag{2}
   \]

   not the reverse. Longer exact identities may merge any number of the
   finite-congruence classes certified here.
4. The numerical finite-SFT pressure ratio \(B_L/Q\) decreases monotonically
   over the tested cutoffs:

   \[
   2.5430,2.2775,2.1400,2.0150,1.8954,1.7867,
   1.6894,1.6099,1.5407
   \]

   for \(L=6,\ldots,14\). This trend does not certify an infinite-cutoff
   limit above or below \(Q\).

Thus all currently enumerated short equal-map relations jointly fail to
compress the canonical language to the target scale. The full all-relations
question and the true F2 mass gate remain open. This lane does not use or
address F3.

## 1. Orientation and exact relation system

Words are outermost first. For \(w=w_1\cdots w_n\), write

\[
 L_w=L_{w_1}\circ\cdots\circ L_{w_n},\qquad
 L_w(t)=M(w)t+d(w),
\]

where

\[
 d(w)=\sum_{i=1}^n q_{w_i}\prod_{h<i}w_h,qquad
 (q_2,q_3,q_5)=(0,1,3).                              \tag{3}
\]

The slope \(M(w)\) is determined by the content of \(w\). Therefore two
equal-content words represent the same affine map exactly when their integer
offsets in (3) agree.

C19 writes the original \(T_j(x)=jx-1\) words in application order. Reversal
puts its map identities in the convention above. In particular its first map
collision becomes

\[
 232552\equiv552223,\qquad
 L_{232552}(t)=L_{552223}(t)=600t+218.                \tag{4}
\]

For a cutoff \(L\), let \(\mathcal R_L\) contain every pair \(u=v\) such
that

\[
 |u|=|v|\le L,\qquad \operatorname{cont}(u)=
 \operatorname{cont}(v),\qquad d(u)=d(v).
\]

Let \(\equiv_L\) be the smallest two-sided word congruence containing
\(\mathcal R_L\). This is the joint contextual quotient requested in C41.

Call a word an active side at cutoff \(L\) if it belongs to a non-singleton
fiber occurring in \(\mathcal R_L\). Let \(\mathcal P_L\) be the active
sides which contain no shorter active side as a consecutive factor. A word
avoids \(\mathcal P_L\) exactly when it contains no side of any relation in
\(\mathcal R_L\).

### Lemma 1: finite isolated words

Every word avoiding \(\mathcal P_L\) is an isolated
\(\equiv_L\)-class.

**Proof.** A first contextual rewrite would have to replace a consecutive
occurrence of one side of a generator in \(\mathcal R_L\). No such occurrence
exists. The same is true in either orientation of a symmetric relation, since
every participating side, not only the lexicographically larger side, was
forbidden. \(\square\)

This is the key distinction from C29's one-sided normal-form automaton. C29
forbids 2,454 oriented left sides through length 14 to obtain an upper bound
on map classes. C41 forbids all participating sides and obtains 4,777 minimal
patterns, giving a lower bound on classes of the finite congruence.

### Theorem 2: the \(L\le14\) joint quotient is too large

Let \(C_k^{(14)}\) be the number of \(\equiv_{14}\)-classes in
\(\Omega_k\). Then (1) holds. In particular

\[
                 C_k^{(14)}\ne O(Q^k/\sqrt{k}).       \tag{10}
\]

**Proof.** Multiply every edge multiplicity by \(m\). Exact balance and
strong connectivity give an Eulerian directed multigraph. Every Euler tour
is a cycle in the safe DFA and has content

\[
                  (15\cdot64587m,10\cdot64587m,
                    6\cdot64587m).
\]

Fixing the starting DFA state, a letter word determines its DFA edge
sequence. The BEST theorem, after dividing out permutations of parallel
copies of each edge type, gives a number \(N_m\) of distinct cyclic words
satisfying

\[
 \log N_m
 \ge m\left(\sum_vd_v\log d_v-\sum_en_e\log n_e\right)
       -O(\log m).                                   \tag{11}
\]

Every such word avoids all sides in \(\mathcal R_{14}\), so Lemma 1 makes
each one a separate \(\equiv_{14}\)-class. Subtracting
\(64587m\log Q\) from (11) and using (7)-(8) gives

\[
 \log\frac{C_{64587m}^{(14)}}{Q^{64587m}}
 \ge m\log\Gamma-O(\log m).
\]

Taking \((64587m)\)-th roots proves (1), and (10) follows. \(\square\)

For completeness, the certified starting state is reached from the DFA root
by the safe prefix `222222`. Prefixing an Euler-tour word by `222222` follows
only safe transitions. The tour word is therefore a factor of a
pattern-avoiding word and itself avoids every pattern in
\(\mathcal P_{14}\).

The constructor used a 2,000,007-edge closed sample. Its content deviation
from the canonical ray was \((20264,-12975)\). The exact correction was

\[
 9(-2266,1455)+(130,-120)=(-20264,12975),             \tag{12}
\]

adding 2,190 edges. Equation (12) is search provenance only; the verifier
checks the final circulation directly.

## 5. Why this is not a true-offset lower bound

All words in \(\Omega_k\) have slope \(Q^k\), so true affine-map classes and
true offset classes coincide. Let their number be \(D_k\). Every relation in
\(\mathcal R_{14}\) is a true map identity, hence there is a surjection

\[
     \Omega_k/\!\equiv_{14}\ \longrightarrow
     \{L_w:w\in\Omega_k\}.
\]

This proves (2). A large source quotient can map onto a small target. Thus
the 37,214-edge circulation proves no nontrivial lower bound for \(D_k\).
In particular it does **not** prove

\[
 D_k\gg Q^k/\sqrt{k},\qquad D_k\gg Q^k,
 \qquad\text{or}\qquad D_k>Q^k.                      \tag{13}
\]

A single identity of length greater than 14 can merge classes which are
isolated under \(\equiv_{14}\). No bounded-length completeness theorem is
available.

If every equal-content/equal-map identity at every length is admitted, the
resulting quotient is exactly the set of true affine maps: any two words in
one true fiber are then a generator pair themselves. C41 proves no size bound
for that infinite quotient.

C19 makes the finite-cutoff issue unavoidable, but its exact statement must
be kept straight: Proposition 4.1 gives primitive **paired collision blocks**
at unbounded lengths, not equal-map identities. Those blocks do not
themselves belong to \(\mathcal R_L\). They show that the broader affine
collision language has genuinely unbounded primitive structure. For the
equal-map language used here, C41 enumerates through length 14 but proves no
stabilization or completeness beyond that cutoff. Either fact forbids
identifying the finite quotient with true affine offsets.

Consequently the finite certificate is a route obstruction only:

> All exact equal-map/equal-content identities through length 14, even used
> jointly with arbitrary overlapping contextual rewrites, are insufficient
> to give a quotient upper bound at the \(Q^k/\sqrt{k}\) scale.

## 6. Exact infinite-pressure frontier

For any finite word \(u\), define its full equal-content map multiplicity

\[
 \mu(u)=\#\{v:\operatorname{cont}(v)=\operatorname{cont}(u),
                    \ L_v=L_u\}.
\]

Let

\[
 \mathcal S_k=
 \{w\in\Omega_k:\text{no consecutive factor }u\preceq w
                         \text{ has }\mu(u)>1\}.      \tag{14}
\]

The factor condition is equivalent to \(\mu(w)=1\):

- if a factor \(u\) has an alternative representative, contextual
  replacement gives an alternative representative of \(w\);
- if \(w\) has an alternative representative, then \(w\) itself is a factor
  satisfying \(\mu(w)>1\).

Therefore every word in \(\mathcal S_k\) is a singleton true affine-map
fiber, and

\[
                         D_k\ge|\mathcal S_k|.         \tag{15}
\]

The exact missing statement for upgrading C41 to a true-class result is:

### Infinite-pressure lemma (open)

There exists \(\eta>0\) such that

\[
 \boxed{
 \liminf_{k\to\infty}\frac1k\log|\mathcal S_k|
       \ge \log Q+\eta.}
                                                               \tag{IP}
\]

Equivalently, the canonical directional pressure of the language avoiding
**all** equal-map relation sides, at unbounded lengths, is strictly larger
than \(Q\). By (15), (IP) would give an exponential true-offset lower bound
and hence the F2 mass gate. The weaker estimate

\[
                         |\mathcal S_k|\gg Q^k/\sqrt{k}          \tag{IP'}
\]

would already suffice for that gate.

A route-specific tail form can use the exact circulation. Let
\(\mathcal T_m\) be its Markov-type words at canonical index \(64587m\).
It would be enough to prove

\[
 \liminf_{m\to\infty}\frac1{64587m}
 \log\frac{|\mathcal T_m\cap\mathcal S_{64587m}|}
               {|\mathcal T_m|}
 >-\log(1.2302522622241512).                         \tag{IP-tail}
\]

No estimate of the survival fraction in (IP-tail) is proved here. The
finite pressure table supplies no uniform control of relation sides whose
length grows with \(k\).

## 7. Prior-art and route comparison

The current Erdos Problems page lists #424 as open and has no partial-solution
comments. Shamazov and Talambutsa,
[On orbit sets generated by semigroups of one-dimensional affine functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06875),
prove orbit lower bounds under freeness and the critical equality
\(\sum_i1/a_i=1\). Here the generators are related and

\[
                         \frac12+\frac13+\frac15=\frac{31}{30}>1.
\]

Kolpakov and Talambutsa,
[On free semigroups of affine maps on the real line](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.09387),
study freeness criteria, not the fixed-content finite-relation pressure
computed here. The novelty recheck found no published all-side quotient
entropy result for these three maps.

Relative to the supplied lanes:

- C19 supplies the exact map census through length 12 and the unbounded
  primitive paired-block warning.
- C29 supplies the one-sided rewrite upper language through length 14.
- C35 shows that one equal-map relation creates exponentially large fibers.
- C36 proves that the full congruence generated by that one relation remains
  exponentially above \(Q^k\).
- C41 includes every exact equal-map relation through length 14 and proves
  the analogous finite-congruence obstruction for their joint action.

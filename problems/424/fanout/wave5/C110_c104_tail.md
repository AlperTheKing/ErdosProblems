# C110: moving-cutoff power tail for the C104 residual roots

## Verdict

No proof or exact falsifier of `C104-BIN` was found.  The deliverable is the
following **strictly weaker sufficient tail lemma**, together with a
reproducible exact gate:

\[
 N_j(X,D):=\#\{r\in\mathcal R_{X,D}:2^j\le r-1<2^{j+1}\},
\]

\[
 \boxed{
 j\ge \lceil\sqrt D\rceil
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 D\,N_j(X,D)^2\le 2^{2j}.}
 \tag{C110-PT}
\]

If `C110-PT` holds uniformly for all integer `X,D>=1`, then the C99/C85
route proves positive lower density.  This implication is proved below.
`C110-PT` itself is not proved.

The exact endpoint gate passes through `X=3,000,000,000` for every
nonempty threshold `1<=D<=17`.  The largest tested ratio

\[
 {D N_j(X,D)^2\over 2^{2j}}
\]

is exactly `275/4096`.  This is finite evidence only.

No boundedness of the source load of a fixed root is assumed.  The bins below
the moving cutoff are paid for by their total reciprocal capacity, so every
fixed bin may eventually contain roots of arbitrarily large source load.

## 1. Why the weaker lemma suffices

Let

\[
 \Sigma_D(X)=\sum_{r\in\mathcal R_{X,D}}{1\over r-1},
 \qquad J=\lceil\sqrt D\rceil.
\]

For a root in bin `j`, `1/(r-1)<=2^{-j}`.  Independently of the closure,
there are at most `2^j` possible integer denominators in that bin.  Hence the
bins `j<J` contribute at most `J` to `Sigma_D(X)`.

For `j>=J`, `C110-PT` gives

\[
 {N_j(X,D)\over2^j}\le {1\over\sqrt D}.
\]

There are at most `1+floor(log_2 X)` relevant bins.  Therefore

\[
 \Sigma_D(X)
 \le J+{1+\lfloor\log_2X\rfloor\over\sqrt D},
\]

and

\[
 {\Sigma_D(X)\over D}
 \le {\lceil\sqrt D\rceil\over D}
   +{1+\lfloor\log_2X\rfloor\over D^{3/2}}.       \tag{1}
\]

Choose

\[
 D=\lfloor(\log X)^c\rfloor,
 \qquad {2\over3}<c<\log2.
\]

The interval is nonempty.  The first term of (1) is `O(D^{-1/2})`, and the
second is

\[
 O((\log X)^{1-3c/2})=o(1).
\]

C99 Theorem C99.3 makes the sources with `d(h)<=D` sparse for every
`c<log 2`.  C99 Proposition C99.5 absorbs the structural splitless roots
because `c>1/2`.  Equation (1) absorbs the reducible-root union.  Substitution
in C85 equation (27) gives `H(X)=o(X)`, hence the existing contraction route
proves positive lower density.

`C110-PT` is strictly weaker than `C104-BIN`.  For example, at `D=4,j=2`,
the formal count `N_2=2` satisfies `C110-PT` with equality, while
`C104-BIN` would require `4*2<=4`, which is false.

More generally, a tail exponent

\[
 N_j(X,D)\le C{2^j\over D^\alpha}
 \quad(j\ge J(D))
\]

is sufficient whenever `J(D)=o(D)` and
`alpha>1/log(2)-1`.  The choice above is `alpha=1/2` and
`J(D)=ceil(sqrt(D))`.

## 2. Source-hypergraph obstruction

The most direct local square-root mechanism is false.  Let `A(h)` be the
number of all witness roots of a hard source and `M(h)` the number of its
reducible witness roots.  The candidates

\[
 M(h)^2\ge d(h)-1
\]

and the same inequality whenever a source upgrades a reducible root both
fail first at

\[
 h=1154,
 \qquad h+1=1155.
\]

Its four admissible pairs are

```text
(5,231), (11,105), (15,77), (33,35).
```

Their witness roots are `116,6,8,18`; only `116` is reducible.  Thus
`d(h)=4`, `M(h)=1`, and `M(h)^2=1<3=d(h)-1`.  The full certificate includes
every missing endpoint and root state.  The coarser claim `A(h)^2>=d(h)`
already fails at `h=6140`, where both pairs collapse to root `12`.

The mixed candidate `A(h)M(h)>=d(h)-1` has no failure through `10^6`, but no
argument was found that converts it into a dyadic tail bound.  No theorem is
claimed for it.

## 3. Exact plus-prime lift lemma

There is an exact way to search for a fixed-root falsifier without assuming
that fixed-root loads are bounded.

Let `N=h+1=1 mod 3` be a hard product, and let `q=1 mod 3` be a prime not
dividing `N`.  Every admissible pair `{a,b}` of `N` produces exactly the two
admissible pairs

\[
 \{a,qb\},\qquad\{qa,b\}
\]

of `qN`.  If both `a,b` are missing, both lifted pairs remain blocked.  If
exactly one endpoint `m` is missing and the other is generated, both lifted
pairs are blocked if and only if `qm` is missing.  Consequently:

**Lemma C110.1.**  The product `qN` is hard if and only if `qm` is missing
for every unique missing endpoint `m` of a complementary pair of `N`.
When this holds, `d(qN)=2d(N)`, and every witness root exposed by a base
missing endpoint remains a witness root.

This is a divisor bijection and uses no asymptotic or fixed-root hypothesis.
The implementation checks the blocker criterion and then independently
re-enumerates every pair of each hit.

The exact search tested all eligible plus primes `q<=100000` for five bases,
including the `d=12` and `d=16` witnesses of roots `54` and `62`.  There was
no lift hit.  The strongest partial rows were:

| `N` | root | `d(N)` | forced blockers | largest number still missing |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 7,634,275 | 54 | 12 | 9 | 7 |
| 2,778,055 | 62 | 12 | 11 | 6 |
| 1,559,219,515 | 54 | 16 | 13 | 6 |
| 298,274,515 | 62 | 16 | 15 | 8 |
| 2,796,867,115 | 54 | 16 | 16 | 7 |

This search neither proves fixed-root boundedness nor excludes a larger
fixed-root counterexample.

## 4. Exact power-tail gate

The direct gate reads the exact threshold counts `N_j(X,D)` and checks

```text
j >= ceil(sqrt(D))  ==>  D*N_j(X,D)^2 <= 2^(2*j)
```

using integer arithmetic.  At `X=3,000,000,000` it checks all
`1<=D<=17`, has maximum source pair count `18`, and returns

```text
all_pointwise_C1_pass = true
largest_pointwise_square_ratio = 275/4096
```

Normal and optimized postprocessing are byte-identical.  The input census
also records no eventwise `C104-BIN` failure through the same cutoff; since
`C104-BIN` implies `C110-PT`, this indirectly checks every preceding source
event.  Neither finite statement is a uniform proof.

A separate C110 scanner checks C104-BIN, the stronger integrated load, and
an aggregate Carleson relaxation event by event through `2*10^9`.  It has no
failure and reproduces the independent classification digest
`4e6f5692a7d4c529`.  An independently written Python implementation matches
all threshold loads, first-failure fields, and root-`54`/`62` blocker profiles
through `300000`; normal and optimized outputs are byte-identical.

## 6. Precise status

`C104-BIN` remains open.  The source-local square-root injection is exactly
false, and the fixed-root plus-prime search produced no counterexample.
`C110-PT` is a strictly weaker arithmetic target with a complete implication
to C99 and an exact finite gate; proving it uniformly would solve the current
residual-root tail frontier.

# Counterexamples and obstructions

- The claim that almost every positive integer occurs is false: no element
  of the closure is congruent to 1 modulo 3.
- Finite gaps or declining empirical density do not imply zero lower density.


- The frozen affine `{2,3,5}` subsystem decreases in density every decade through `10^11`; it is not an established positive-density subproblem.
- B03 proves no finite certificate based on globally disjoint images of whole residue classes can be critical. Orbit-relative overlap control is required.

- Raw word counting is false: `T_322255(x)=T_255232(x)=600x-381`.
- A generic polynomial-growth bootstrap is false. The distinct-input closure
  of seeds `{9,10}` has a proved polynomial lower bound and a sublinear upper
  bound, despite making every generated value available as a multiplier.
- No nonempty periodic set `P` satisfies
  `P subset union_(d in D)(dP-1)` for finite `D`; eventual-full-residue
  strong-induction certificates are impossible.
- Periodic composition domains face the necessary gate
  `exists d in D: d divides gcd(q,r+2)` on every residue `qZ+r`.

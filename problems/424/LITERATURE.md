# Literature audit

## Primary formulation

- P. Erdos, “Problems and results on combinatorial number theory III,”
  *Number Theory Day*, LNM 626 (1977), pp. 43–72, DOI
  10.1007/BFb0063064, printed p. 71.
- The rendered primary scan says “products of two distinct elements,”
  subtract 1, repeat indefinitely, and asks whether the sequence has positive
  density. It attributes the problem to D. Hofstadter.
- The source does not define “positive density.” The sprint deliberately
  asks the stronger explicit liminf question.
- Distinct values, not merely distinct list positions, are load-bearing:
  the latter convention would insert 24 from two copies of 5, whereas the
  source-faithful distinct-value closure omits 24.

The scan SHA-256 and visual transcription are recorded in
`fanout/wave1/A01_primary_erdos1977.md`.

## Later formulations

- Erdos and Graham, *Old and New Problems and Results in Combinatorial Number
  Theory* (1980), p. 84, gives a later version. Its density-one or “almost all”
  wording is false for the distinct-value closure because residue 1 mod 3 is
  absent.
- R. K. Guy, *Unsolved Problems in Number Theory*, E31, repeats the problem.
- B. Green, *100 Open Problems*, Problem 63, records the product-minus-one
  closure and the large-seed zero-density phenomenon.
- OEIS A005244 records finite terms; it supplies no density theorem.
- The Erdos Problems page #424 records the distinct-input convention and open
  status. The modulo-3 obstruction is attributed there to Stefan
  Steinerberger.

## Closely related theorems audited

- Shamazov and Talambutsa, “On orbit sets generated by semigroups of
  one-dimensional affine functions,” arXiv:2507.06875; *Expositiones
  Mathematicae* 44(3) (2026), 125765.
  Their exact-covering-system theorem gives positive density only when the
  affine images form a disjoint exact cover. For the induced maps
  `g_d(u)=du+d-2`, every pair of whole-class images intersects, so that
  theorem does not apply. Their free critical-orbit theorem yields only a
  near-linear logarithmic loss and does not handle the present collision
  structure.
- Kim, Yip, and Yoo, “Multiplicative structure of shifted multiplicative
  subgroups and its applications to Diophantine tuples,” *Canadian Journal
  of Mathematics* (2025), DOI 10.4153/S0008414X25000136.
  Their finite-field bound applies only under a power-subgroup containment
  hypothesis and does not control integer lower density.
- Klarner–Rado closure results concern fixed linear operations; the available
  multiplier in #424 is generated dynamically, so those results do not
  transfer.

Exact hypotheses, citations, and non-transfer arguments are in
`fanout/wave1/A04_modern_literature.md`.

## Novelty verdict

No checked primary source, modern theorem, citation trail, exact-phrase search,
or official problem record resolves the distinct-value positive-lower-density
question. This is a documented search result, not a proof that no differently
named result exists.

The modulo-3 obstruction is prior art. The sprint’s quantitative polynomial
lower bound, periodic-decoder obstruction, and exact affine-collision analysis
require broader prior-art review before any novelty claim.

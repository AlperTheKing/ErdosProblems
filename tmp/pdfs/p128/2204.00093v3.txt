Abstract
Denote by qn(G) the smallest eigenvalue of the signless Laplacian matrix of ann-vertex graph
G. Brandt conjectured in 1997 that for regular triangle-free graphs qn(G)≤ 4n
25 . We prove a
stronger result: If G is a triangle-free graph then qn(G)≤ 15n
94 < 4n
25 . Brandt’s conjecture is a
subproblem of two famous conjectures of Erd˝ os:
(1) Sparse-Half-Conjecture: Every n-vertex triangle-free graph has a subset of vertices of
size⌈ n
2⌉ spanning at most n2/50 edges.
(2) Every n-vertex triangle-free graph can be made bipartite by removing at most n2/25
edges.
In our proof we use linear algebraic methods to upper bound qn(G) by the ratio between the
number of induced paths with 3 and 4 vertices. We give an upper bound on this ratio via the
method of ﬂag algebras.

Note that, if G is d-regular, then λ1(G) = d and qn(G) = λn(G) + d = λn(G) + λ1(G). Thus
Theorem 1.6 implies that λ1(G) + λn(G) < 0.1596n < 4n

graph G, conﬁrming Conjecture 1.3 in strong form.
It remains open to determine a sharp upper bound for qn(G)/n for triangle-free n-vertex graph
G. While we only prove Theorem 1.6 with the constant 15

ﬂag algebras to show a slightly stronger bound qn(G) = λ1(G) + λn(G) < 0.15442n. As we believe
neither of these two bounds are sharp (see Section 3), we omit presenting their proofs.

3 Concluding remarks
As we have already mentioned in the introduction, a signiﬁcantly larger ﬂag algebra computation
than the one used in our proof yields that qn(G) < 0.15467n for every triangle-free n-vertex graph.
Similarly, assuming that G is regular allows us to show λ1(G) + λn(G) < 0.15442n. On the other
hand, our method will be able to get neither of the coeﬃcients below 42 /275 = 0.1527.
Indeed, consider the Higman-Sims graph GHS . It is edge-transitive so mxy = 21· 6 + 22 for
every xy∈ E(GHS ), and wx = 222 for every x∈ V (G), where mxy and wx are deﬁned as before
Lemma 2.1. Therefore,

every triangle-free graph G on n vertices.

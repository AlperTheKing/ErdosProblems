Abstract. Erdős conjectured that everyn-vertex triangle-free graph contains a subset
of tn{2u vertices that spans at mostn2{50 edges. Extending a recent result of Norin and
Yepremyan, we conﬁrm this conjecture for graphs homomorphic to so-called Andrásfai
graphs. As a consequence, Erdős’ conjecture holds for every triangle-free graphG with
minimum degreeδpGqą 10n{29 and ifχpGqď 3 the degree condition can be relaxed to
δpGqą n{3. In fact, we obtain a more general result for graphs of higher odd-girth.

Our ﬁrst result validates Conjecture 1.1 (stated in the contrapositive) for graphs homo-

Theorem 1.2. If a graph G is homomorphic to an Andrásfai graph Fd for some inte-

Owing to the work of Chen, Jin, and Koh [4], which asserts that every triangle-free

following corollary.
Corollary 1.3. LetG be a triangle-free graph on n vertices.
(a) If δpGqą 10n{29, then G is not p1{2, 1{50q-dense.
(b) If δpGqą n{3 and χpGqď 3, then G is not p1{2, 1{50q-dense.
We remark that part (a) slightly improves earlier results of Krivelevich [12] and of Norin
and Yepremyan [14] (see also [11] where an average degree condition was considered).

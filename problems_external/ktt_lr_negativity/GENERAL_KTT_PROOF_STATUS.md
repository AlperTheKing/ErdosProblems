# KTT positivity: proved scope and exact general frontier

Date: 2026-07-22

## Status

The full King--Tollu--Toumazet coefficient-positivity conjecture has **not**
been proved.  The following statements have been proved and independently
replayed.

### 1. Complete result through side size four

For every nonzero Littlewood--Richardson triple whose partitions have at most
four parts, the stretching polynomial

```text
P(n) = c(n nu; n lambda, n mu)
```

has nonnegative ordinary monomial coefficients.  In the full-dimensional
side-four case all four coefficients are positive.

The proof is structural rather than a census.  Period-one scaling transfers
the Berline--Vergne local formula from a lattice dilation back to the rational
hive polytope.  In intrinsic dimensions at most two Ehrhart positivity is
universal.  In dimension three, the only nontrivial coefficient is the linear
one; every two-dimensional transverse cone generated by side-four rhombus
normals has positive BV value.  Vertex cones need not be unimodular and are
not used.

Proof: `r4_reeve/R4_STRUCTURAL_PROOF.md`.

### 2. Rank-uniform top-coefficient result

Let a full-dimensional side-`r` hive polytope have intrinsic dimension `D`.
The coefficients of

```text
n^D, n^(D-1), n^(D-2), n^(D-3)
```

are positive.  The first two are the usual volume and boundary terms.  The
last two follow from exact rank-uniform BV positivity for every closed
codimension-two and codimension-three hive normal cone.

Proofs: `UNIFORM_CODIM2_POSITIVITY.md` and
`UNIFORM_CODIM3_BV_REPORT.md`.

### 3. One further coefficient in full-dimensional side five

A full-dimensional side-five hive has dimension six.  Its quadratic
coefficient is positive.  Consequently its coefficients of

```text
n^6, n^5, n^4, n^3, n^2
```

are positive; only the linear coefficient remains open in this class.

The codimension-four proof includes all `C(27,4)=17550` four-normal tuples.
All 903 nonsaturated tuples of indices two, three, or four have positive exact
BV value, with minimum `1/14400`.  There are 132 negative saturated cells.
Exact slack closure either forces normal rank at least five or embeds such a
cell in one of 513 pointed rank-preserving closed supersets; every one of
those full cones is positive, with minimum `739/86400`.

Proof and canonical replays: `R5_CODIM4_LOCAL_POSITIVITY_REPORT.md`.

This does not cover lower-dimensional side-five hives.  The exact triple

```text
lambda = (4,3,3,1,0)
mu     = (4,2,1,1,0)
nu     = (6,5,4,2,2)
```

has hive dimension three while all 142 essential Horn inequalities are
strict.  Therefore Horn-facet factorization does not reduce every
lower-dimensional side-five case to side at most four.  See
`R5_LOWERDIM_HORN_GAP.md`.

## Why the local evidence does not yet prove full KTT

For a period-one hive polytope, the local formula writes each Ehrhart
coefficient as a pairing of face volumes with BV/Todd weights on transverse
normal cones.  At Todd order four, individual saturated cells can already be
negative; one realized cell has value

```text
-349/28800.
```

Forced flat-rhombus closure repairs it in the explicit side-five face, whose
full normal cone has value

```text
17977/604800 > 0.
```

Thus termwise simplex positivity is false.  The side-five theorem succeeds
because all possible closed repairs can still be classified finitely.  Doing
this separately in codimensions five, six, and so on would be an unbounded
certificate cascade, not a general proof.

## Exact lemma that would close the general conjecture

For every rank, codimension `q`, and 2-connected primitive-interior closed
flat-rhombus coarsening `Sigma`, let `a_q` be its vector of BV/Todd weights and
let

```text
partial_q : Q^{Sigma(q)} -> E_q
```

be the Minkowski boundary map.  It would suffice to prove the following
hive-specific effectivity statement:

```text
there exists y such that  a_q + partial_q^T y >= 0.       (HTE)
```

By rational Farkas duality, this is equivalent to

```text
<a_q,w> >= 0
```

for every nonnegative balanced realizable face weight `w`.  Pairing (HTE)
with the positive face-volume weight of any hive polytope gives every
Ehrhart coefficient nonnegative.  Horn factorization handles separating
boundary coarsenings; the load-bearing case is the 2-connected primitive
interior.

No proof of (HTE), and no negative realizable balanced weight refuting it, is
currently available.

## Rank-uniform routes that were exactly obstructed

1. **Generic type-A Todd or cocircuit balancing.**  A nonnegative polynomial
   `p=L^4` in the `A4` Dahmen--Micchelli space is sent by the Todd operator to
   `N^4+4N^3+3N^2-2N-6/5`.  Therefore nonnegativity plus generic type-A
   balancing cannot imply (HTE).  See `PLANAR_TODD_BRIDGE_AUDIT.md`.

2. **Ordinary network-flow equivalence.**  A simple side-four hive tangent
   cone has determinant two, whereas simple ordinary network-flow cones are
   unimodular in their intrinsic flow lattice.  Hive flows retain extra
   rhombus-capacity constraints, so Lidskii positivity does not transfer.
   See `FLOW_BRIDGE_OBSTRUCTION.md`.

3. **Generic Hilbert-ring properties.**  The LR ray algebra is a
   Cohen--Macaulay invariant ring with rational singularities and negative
   `a`-invariant.  These facts do not control monomial coefficients: the
   `Q_20` Hibi ring is standard, normal, CM, rational, Koszul, Gorenstein and
   has `a<0`, but its Hilbert polynomial has linear coefficient
   `-168011/330`.  See `HILBERT_HURWITZ_BRIDGE_REPORT.md`.

4. **Matroidal or secondary-fan Todd effectivity.**  A genuine side-four hive
   face has a `U_{2,4}` tight-normal restriction, so the tight-row system is
   not graphic, cographic, or regular.  Matroid combinatorics also forgets the
   determinant-two lattice data.  Generic permutohedral Todd effectivity and
   matroid Ehrhart positivity are themselves false.  See
   `MATROID_TODD_BRIDGE_AUDIT.md`.

5. **Known positive tableau or representation recursions.**  Fixed LR
   content is a global fiber/coefficient extraction; fermionic configuration
   sets change with `n`; reverse Kostka and branching reductions require
   signs or subtraction; Horn factorization stops on primitive triples.  An
   exact primitive side-five triple already has all 142 essential Horn
   inequalities strict.  See `LR_POSITIVE_RECURSION_AUDIT.md` and its
   erratum.

6. **Hurwitz stability.**  Strict Hurwitz stability would imply KTT because
   real factors and conjugate-pair quadratic factors would all have positive
   coefficients.  The exact LR atlas is consistent with this stronger
   conjecture, but no invariant-theoretic, skep, or recursion theorem implies
   it.  Treating its Hurwitz determinants rank by rank would be another
   unbounded hierarchy, not a proof.

## Honest conclusion

The side-four KTT theorem and the rank-uniform top-four-coefficient theorem
are complete.  Full-dimensional side five is now reduced to its linear
coefficient.  Full KTT remains open.  A legitimate continuation needs a new
rank-uniform proof of (HTE), or an equivalent hive-specific positive
decomposition; the generic geometric, algebraic, flow, matroidal, tableau,
and Horn shortcuts audited above do not supply it.

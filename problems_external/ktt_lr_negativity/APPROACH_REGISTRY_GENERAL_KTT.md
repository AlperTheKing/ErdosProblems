## Exact target

For every nonzero Littlewood--Richardson triple `(lambda, mu, nu)` in every
rank, prove that the stretching polynomial

```text
P(n) = c(n nu; n lambda, n mu)
```

has nonnegative coefficients in the ordinary monomial basis.  If the
conjecture is false, the alternative successful deliverable is one exact,
independently verified counterexample certificate.

## DIRECT ROUTE — CLOSED HIVE TODD DELETION

### 1. Exact final deliverable

A publication-grade rank-uniform proof of the King--Tollu--Toumazet
coefficient-positivity conjecture.  The proof must cover every rank, every
intrinsic dimension, and every coefficient.  A cascade of fixed-rank or
fixed-codimension certificates is not a deliverable.

The permitted alternative is a finite counterexample certificate containing
one partition triple, its exact stretching polynomial over `Q`, two
independent exact counting engines through the degree bound plus two held-out
dilations, and an independently checked negative coefficient.

### 2. Current frontier lemma

`CLOSED-HIVE-TODD-DELETION`.

For every codimension `q` and every 2-connected primitive-interior closed
flat-rhombus coarsening `Sigma`, let

```text
W_q(Sigma) = {w >= 0 : partial_q w = 0}
```

be its cone of nonnegative balanced realizable face weights, and let `a_q` be
the Berline--Vergne/Todd weight vector.  Prove that `Sigma` admits a
rank-reducing local deletion or contraction `Sigma -> Sigma'` for which

1. `W_q(Sigma)` is generated by lifts of `W_q(Sigma')` together with a
   rank-independent finite family of local star/corridor weights; and
2. the Todd pairing is nonnegative on every new local generator and differs
   from the lifted pairing only by explicitly nonnegative local corrections.

Equivalently, prove inductively that for every such `Sigma` there exists a
rational vector `y` satisfying

```text
a_q + partial_q^T y >= 0.                              (HTE)
```

### 3. Explicit logical bridge to full KTT

By rational Farkas duality, `(HTE)` is equivalent to

```text
<a_q, w> >= 0  for every w in W_q(Sigma).
```

The relative face-volume vector of every hive polytope is a nonnegative
balanced realizable weight.  The Berline--Vergne local Euler--Maclaurin
formula therefore expresses each Ehrhart coefficient as a nonnegative Todd
pairing.  Polynomiality supplies period one, and denominator clearing
transfers the lattice formula to rational hive PIPs.  Thus `(HTE)` in every
codimension proves every monomial coefficient nonnegative in every rank.

Separating Horn-boundary coarsenings factor.  The deletion lemma is only
load-bearing on the 2-connected primitive-interior closed case.

### 4. Next falsifiable action

Build the exact chain-level model for `partial_q`, closed rhombus flats, and
Todd weights under one planar ear/strip deletion.  In parallel:

1. derive the exact lift and local correction matrices symbolically;
2. prove or falsify that every minimal 2-connected closed coarsening has an
   admissible rank-reducing ear/strip; and
3. enumerate only the smallest realizable closed coarsenings needed to
   falsify the proposed move set, searching for either a no-deletion
   coarsening or a rational `w >= 0` with `partial_q w=0` and
   `<a_q,w><0`.

Finite enumeration is a falsification gate only, never the proof.  A
surviving move set must be proved rank-uniformly by planar structure and exact
lattice identities.

### 5. Exit condition

Declare this route

```text
DEAD: closed-hive deletion does not imply HTE — <exact obstruction>
```

on the first reproducible occurrence of any of the following:

1. an actual closed primitive-interior hive coarsening with no admissible
   rank-reducing move;
2. a nonnegative balanced realizable weight with negative Todd pairing;
3. a deletion whose correction necessarily has a negative generator; or
4. a move family whose types or proof obligations grow with rank, producing
   an unbounded fixed-codimension hierarchy.

On exit, preserve the obstruction and return only to another registry route
with a complete logical bridge to full KTT.  Do not continue a rank or
codimension census.

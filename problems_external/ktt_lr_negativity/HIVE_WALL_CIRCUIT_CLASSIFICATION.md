# Actual hive support walls are not uniformly unit `2<->2` circuits

Date: 2026-07-22

## Verdict

The proposed rank-uniform classification is false in the natural support-fan
sense, already in the smallest possible rank, `r=4`.

There is an exact adjacent pair of full-dimensional side-four hive polytopes
whose complete normal fans differ by a facet birth.  After quotienting either
actual common link, the primitive circuit is

```text
a + b = c,
```

with sign split `2<->1`, not `2<->2`.  All three coefficients are units, so
the failure is genuinely the sign pattern; it is not caused by a hidden
lattice index.

This is a wall-classification counterexample only.  It is not a
counterexample to GHTE, KTT, or coefficient positivity.

## 1. The fixed `A_4` circuit and the restricted support functional

Use the standard three interior hive coordinates in the ordering

```text

```

```text

```

They form the primitive minimal dependence

```text

```

For a boundary triple `p=(lambda,mu,nu)`, let `h_i(p)` be the support

`|lambda|+|mu|=|nu|`, the matching circuit functional is exactly

```text
Omega(p) = h_1(p)+h_3(p)-h_7(p)
         = nu_2-lambda_4-mu_1.                         (2)
```

Thus this raw circuit is not killed by the restricted boundary/support map.
Equation (2) gives an explicit transverse boundary coordinate.

## 3. Exact complete-fan change

Put

```text
u=(0,0,1),        v=(1,0,-1),        e=(1,0,0).
```

Exact cddlib canonicalization gives

```text
facets(L) = {a,b,u,v,e},
facets(R) = {a,b,c,u,v,e}.                              (7)
```

Both polytopes are full-dimensional and simple.  Their complete fans have
six and eight maximal cones respectively.  Four maximal cones are unchanged;
the entire difference is

```text
L only:  abu,  abv,
R only:  acu,  bcu,  acv,  bcv.                        (8)
```

Thus the `R` fan is the star subdivision of the two-cone `cone(a,b)` in the
`L` fan by the new primitive ray

```text
c=a+b.                                                   (9)
```

The two actual common links are `u` and `v`.  Quotienting them does not alter
the circuit:

```text
q_u(x,y,z)=(x,y),
q_v(x,y,z)=(x+z,y).
```

Both maps are surjective lattice maps with saturated kernel generated by the
named primitive link.  Under either map,

```text
q(a)=(-1,1),   q(b)=(0,-1),   q(c)=(-1,0),
q(a)+q(b)=q(c).                                         (10)
```

Consequently (1) remains the primitive quotient-lattice circuit with
coefficients `(1,1,-1)`.  The `2<->1` conclusion survives the exact intrinsic
link quotient required by GHTE.

## 4. Why rank four is minimal

The number of interior coordinates of a side-`r` hive is

```text
d_r=(r-1)(r-2)/2.
```

For `r<=2`, `d_r=0`.  For `r=3`, `d_r=1`; the only distinct primitive normal
ray directions are `+1` and `-1`.  Their dependence is positive and hence
nonpointed, so making both sides tight collapses intrinsic dimension rather
than producing two adjacent full-dimensional normal fans.  A switch between
parallel copies of the same ray does not change the complete normal fan and
is not a GHTE wall-transport move.

Therefore `r=4` is the first rank in which a genuine complete-fan support
wall can occur, and (3)--(10) show that the proposed classification already
fails there.

## 5. Precise surviving hypothesis and limitation

If one restricts a priori to an **elementary facet-preserving side-four
wall**, all circuit rays must remain facets on both sides.  A `1<->k` circuit
is then excluded because its singleton-side ray is redundant on the opposite
side.  In dimension three, a full-rank elementary circuit has four rays, so
having at least two signs on each side forces a `2<->2` sign split.

This observation is only a side-four, facet-preserving statement.  It does
not prove that the coefficients are units in arbitrary rank, and it does not
exclude longer circuits in higher-dimensional hives.  The exact `r=4`
`2<->2` wall from `ghte_find_r4_wall_pair.py` is one instance, not a
rank-uniform classification theorem.

Accordingly, the phrase "every hive wall is a unit `2<->2` flip" can be used
only after explicitly assuming all of the following local data:

1. both chambers have the identical facet-normal set;
2. the elementary closed circuit has exactly four rays after the saturated
   intrinsic/link quotient;
3. its sign split is `2+2`; and
4. its primitive quotient coefficients are all units.

Those are hypotheses to verify, not consequences of the fixed matrix `A_r`
and the boundary map.

## 6. Explicit bridge to GHTE wall transport

The counterwall identifies an additional mandatory transport move.  In the
orientation (7)--(9), `Sigma_R` refines `Sigma_L`.  The already proved
refinement-descent lemma gives the one-way implication

```text
GHTE(Sigma_R)  =>  GHTE(Sigma_L).                       (11)
```

It does not give the reverse implication.  Moreover, the exact follow-up
audit `GHTE_ACTUAL_STAR_SUBDIVISION_TODD_OBSTRUCTION.md` computes

```text
t_q(Sigma_R)-pi^*t_q(Sigma_L)
  = (0,-[D_c]/2,-(5/6)[V(cu)],0),   q=0,1,2,3.         (12)
```

Strictly positive balanced weights pair with the two nonzero corrections as
`-1/2` and `-5/6`.  Hence the canonical upward pullback cannot be repaired by
a nonnegative Farkas correction.  Both endpoint fans nevertheless satisfy
GHTE independently, so (12) is a transport obstruction only.

Therefore a rank-uniform propagation argument would require a different
hive-specific positive map for actual star subdivisions, in addition to any
facet-preserving `2<->2` transport.  A theorem covering only unit `2<->2`
flips cannot connect all adjacent hive support chambers.

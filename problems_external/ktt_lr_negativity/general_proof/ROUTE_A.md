## 1. Step (1), executed: primitivity does NOT give a stronger positivity criterion — it CONCENTRATES the danger

The corpus is the 2492 verified triples of `hstar_spread/hstar_atlas2.tsv`
(r=4..7, d=3..10, M up to 682 020), each with stored, held-out-verified h\* and
recorded λ;μ;ν.  Classifying every one:

```

triples with any NEGATIVE Ehrhart coefficient (R_k > 1): 0
```

**Closest-to-negative ratio maxR = max_k R_k, split by primitivity:**

```
PRIMITIVE     n=2258  maxR = 0.98710   #R>.90 = 209   #R>.95 = 36
NONPRIMITIVE  n= 234  maxR = 0.90142   #R>.90 =   1   #R>.95 =  0
```

Every near-miss is primitive.  Non-primitive (factorable) triples stay bounded
away from the threshold (maxR ≤ 0.901); the entire dangerous population sits in
the primitive class.  So Route A's reduction to the primitive case lands the
whole problem **exactly on the danger zone**: primitivity does not *exclude*
near-negativity, it *is* where near-negativity concentrates.

**The literal step-(1) target confirms this.**  F1 gives `a_{d−2} < 0 ⟺
⟨u²⟩ < (d+1)/3` and `a_{d−1} < 0 ⟺ ⟨u⟩ > 0`.  Over the corpus:

```
⟨u²⟩ − (d+1)/3 :  primitive min = +0.1771   nonprimitive min = +0.6667   (never < 0)
⟨u⟩           :  primitive max = −0.3750   (never > 0)
```

`⟨u²⟩ ≥ (d+1)/3` holds — but it is **not** a new fact and **not** primitive-
specific: `a_{d−2} ≥ 0` and `a_{d−1} ≥ 0` are already proven *rank-uniformly*
for all hive polytopes (`UNIFORM_CODIM2_POSITIVITY.md`,
`UNIFORM_CODIM3_BV_REPORT.md`, the top-four-coefficient theorem).  Moreover the
primitive margin (0.177) is *smaller* than the non-primitive margin (0.667):
primitivity pushes every top-coefficient moment **toward** its threshold.  The
hoped-for "stronger Ehrhart-positivity criterion from primitivity" does not
exist.  **Step (1)'s hypothesis is falsified.**

## 5. Why no h\*-SHAPE property can be the saving lemma (exact obstruction)

It is tempting to hope the observed regularity (h\* unimodal — holds for
2254/2258 primitives, the 4 exceptions being the trivial M = 2 slabs — with the
dangerous cases centre-peaked) forces `a_1 ≥ 0`.  It cannot.  Stanley's order
polytope `O_{P_{7,7}}` (the ordinal sum of two 7-antichains; Liu–Tsuchiya
arXiv:1806.08403) has h\* = A_7(z)·A_7(z) (product of Eulerian polynomials),
which is **palindromic, unimodal, and centre-peaked**, dimension 14, and yet

```
a_1(O_{P_{7,7}}) = −3041/1430 < 0     (reproduced exactly here from its h*)
```

So a lattice polytope with unimodal + palindromic + centre-peaked h\* can have a
negative linear coefficient.  No property of the *shape* of the h\*-vector
(unimodality, symmetry, peak location) can force `a_1 ≥ 0`.  Whatever protects
hive polytopes is **quantitative and hive-specific** — precisely the effectivity
statement (HTE) already identified as the general frontier in
`GENERAL_KTT_PROOF_STATUS.md`.  Step (1) can therefore not be upgraded to a new
positivity criterion by any moment- or shape-based argument.

Regarding step (2): re-deriving BV/McMullen locality for primitive r=5 with
non-unimodular cones is exactly the codim-4 program already carried out in
`R5_CODIM4_LOCAL_POSITIVITY_REPORT.md` (top five coefficients of full-dim side
five proven positive; a_1 the sole residual).  The present analysis shows the
residual a_1 is the §3 knife-edge and that no shape lemma closes it.

## Verdict

**BLOCKED.**  Route A's next step, executed in full, produces neither a proof
nor a counterexample.  It (i) *falsifies* the step-(1) hypothesis — primitivity
gives no stronger Ehrhart-positivity criterion; it concentrates the danger; (ii)

r.  Decisive falsifying fact for step (1): the primitive corpus reaches
R = 0.98710 while the non-primitive corpus caps at 0.90142, so primitivity does
the opposite of what step (1) hoped.

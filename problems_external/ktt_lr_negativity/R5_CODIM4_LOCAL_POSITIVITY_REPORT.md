# Rank-five codimension-four BV locality audit

Date: 2026-07-22

## Verdict

Let `P` be a full-dimensional side-five hive polytope (ambient/intrinsic
dimension six), and let `F` be any two-dimensional face.  Then

```text
alpha^BV(F,P) > 0.
```

Consequently the quadratic Ehrhart coefficient of every full-dimensional
rank-five hive polytope with a period-one Ehrhart quasipolynomial is positive.
For stretched Littlewood--Richardson polynomials, period one is supplied by
polynomiality.  The rational-polytope passage uses the separate scaling lemma:
clear a denominator `q`, apply the lattice local formula to `qP`, and compare
`L_(qP)(n)=L_P(qn)`; normal cones are unchanged and normalized two-face areas
scale by `q^2`.

This is not the full KTT conjecture.  In full rank five it settles the `n^2`
coefficient; the linear coefficient remains outside this audit.  Lower-
dimensional rank-five hives also require an intrinsic-lattice contraction
argument and are not claimed here.

## Why the first negative result was not a counterexample

The defining local Euler--Maclaurin recursion independently finds 132 negative
saturated four-normal cells among the rank-five atlas.  The minimum is

```text
alpha = -66821/2858240
normal IDs = (0,7,23,25)
original rows = (12,14,10,19)
```

This simplicial cone cannot be the full normal cone of a codimension-four hive
face.  If `s_i` denotes rhombus slack, the exact identities

```text
s_3 + s_4  = s_10 + s_14
s_15 + s_3 = s_10 + s_12
```

show that `s_10=s_12=s_14=s_19=0` forces rows `3,4,15` tight as well.  The
seven forced row normals have rank five, so the proposed codimension-four face
does not exist.  No numerical tolerance is used in these identities.

Other negative cells can close to a genuine rank-four nonsimplicial cone.  For
example the integral boundary

```text
lambda = (11,8,6,2,0)
mu     = (10,8,4,2,0)
nu     = (16,13,10,8,4)
```

has a full-dimensional hive with a two-face whose exactly active rows are

```text
(1,2,8,9,11,12,15).
```

An exact face point and strict interior point are

```text
x = (24,32,36,37,43,46)
z = (26,33,36,37,43,46),
```

with every required nonzero slack at least one.  In the saturated normal basis
with active normal IDs `(0,1,2,3,4,5)`, direct normal-cone subdivision gives
three saturated maximal cells with values

```text
39/3200,  -349/28800,  3587/120960.
```

Berline--Vergne's dual-normal-cone functional is a simple valuation under a
normal-cone subdivision (Berline--Vergne, Corollary 24), so the full value is

```text
39/3200 - 349/28800 + 3587/120960 = 17977/604800 > 0.
```

An independent subdivision of the polar feasible cone, with the required
internal-face inclusion--exclusion, gives the same fraction.  The two
subdivision conventions must not be mixed: maximal-cell summation is the
simple valuation on the **normal** cone.

<https://nicole.berline.perso.math.cnrs.fr/Berline-Vergne-Local%20Euler%20Maclaurin.pdf>

## Exhaustive coverage argument

The side-five atlas has 27 distinct oriented primitive normals in `Z^6`.
There are `C(27,4)=17550` four-tuples:

```text
dependent : 3102
index 1   : 13545
index 2   : 885
index 3   : 15
index 4   : 3
```

The coverage proof is as follows.

1. Every rank-four pointed normal cone generated by hive normals has a
   triangulation using its extreme hive-normal rays.  Berline--Vergne
   Corollary 24 expresses its value as the sum of the values of the maximal
   simplicial normal cells.

2. All 903 nonsaturated independent four-tuples were saturated explicitly by
   exact coset-ray star subdivisions.  Every value is strictly positive; the
   minimum is `1/14400`.

3. Of the 13,545 saturated four-tuples, exactly 132 have negative value and
   none has value zero.  Hence a normal cone with no one of these 132 cells in
   a triangulation is automatically positive.

4. Expand the 132 dangerous normal tuples through duplicated original
   rhombus rows: there are 192 row tuples.  Exact Farkas closure forces normal
   rank at least five in 54 cases, excluding codimension four.  The other 138
   occurrences reduce to 96 distinct minimal forced rank-four cones.

5. An actual face may have further tight rows not universally forced.  Such a
   row must have its normal in the same rank-four span.  Every subset of all 27
   atlas directions in those spans was adjoined to every minimal closure.  The
   resulting over-approximation has 9,888 generated occurrences and 4,608
   distinct direction sets.  Of these, 4,095 are nonpointed and cannot be a
   face normal cone.  All 513 pointed full cones are strictly positive; their
   minimum is `739/86400`.

Thus, if a triangulation has no negative cell, step 3 proves positivity.  If it
has a negative cell, steps 4--5 contain the entire actual normal cone and prove
its positivity.  These alternatives exhaust every full-dimensional rank-five
codimension-four hive face.

## Superseded intermediate failures

Two intermediate drivers are intentionally not canonical:

- `r5_codim4_negative_realizability_audit.py` used the incorrect expected
  duplicate-row expansion count `164`; the exact count is `192`.
- `r5_codim4_all_rankpreserving_supersets.py` stopped on a determinant-two
  subdivision cell with indices `(1,1,1,2)`.  The canonical `_v2` driver
  inserts exact fundamental-parallelepiped coset rays and verifies that all
  final maximal cells are saturated.  Across the full superset audit it
  inserted 101 such rays.

These failures are preserved because they document the two places where an
uncertified shortcut would have invalidated the coverage claim.

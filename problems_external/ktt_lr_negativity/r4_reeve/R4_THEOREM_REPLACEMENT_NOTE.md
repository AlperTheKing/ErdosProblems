# Replacement note for the rank-four proof

`R4_KTT_THEOREM.md` and `R4_EDGE_POSITIVITY_CERTIFICATE.md` gave a valid but
unnecessarily elaborate route through a 72-witness balance-space
certificate and a separate vertex-integrality input.

The canonical rank-four proof is now `R4_STRUCTURAL_PROOF.md`, derived as the
rank-four corollary of `../UNIFORM_CODIM2_POSITIVITY.md`.

The old finite certificate remains an independently replayable artifact, but
it is **not load-bearing** for the theorem.  The replacement proof:

1. extends the local formula from lattice polytopes to rational polytopes
   whose Ehrhart count is an ordinary polynomial by scaling `qP`;
2. proves every two-dimensional transverse feasible cone generated by hive
   rhombus normals has positive Berline--Vergne constant; and
3. handles actual hive dimensions at most two by the scaled segment and
   polygon formulas.

Thus Buch's rank-four vertex-integrality proposition and the 72-witness
certificate are both dispensable for KTT positivity in rank at most four.

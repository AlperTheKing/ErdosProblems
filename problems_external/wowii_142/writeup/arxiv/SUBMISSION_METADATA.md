## Abstract

For a finite simple connected graph G, let t(G) be the largest order of an induced subgraph that is a tree, let g(G) be the girth, let Per(G) be the set of peripheral vertices, and put f(G)=max_x d(x,Per(G)). We prove t(G) >= (2/3)g(G)+f(G), which is Conjecture 142 of the conjecture-making program Graffiti.pc (DeLaVina, Written on the Wall II, 2005). In the cyclic case we prove the stronger integral form t(G) >= f(G)+ceil(2g(G)/3). The main ingredient is a rooted shortest-cycle lemma: in a graph of girth at least five, any three prescribed vertices can be placed in a tree obtained from a shortest cycle by deleting one cycle vertex and attaching an admissible induced forest. A three-point metric estimate then reduces the result to exact arithmetic, with one extremal family handled separately.
